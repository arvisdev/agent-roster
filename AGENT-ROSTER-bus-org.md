# AGENT ROSTER (bus-org)

Business organization — intent, requirements, risk, and compliance. These agents
turn stakeholder intent into spec-ready, compliance-checked requirements and gate
the handoff to the EA org.

Behavioral Specification Specialist

Agent: https://github.com/arvisdev/agent-roster/tree/main/bus-org/behavioral-specification-specialist
Description: Specialist that turns spec-ready requirements into independently testable sub-behavior specs (BDD/Gherkin, acceptance criteria, traceability) — pick when a requirement must become a testable behavioral spec
Model: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash
Image: hermes-agent

Business Requirements Specialist

Agent: https://github.com/arvisdev/agent-roster/tree/main/bus-org/business-requirements-specialist
Description: Business Analyst that specializes in building and gathering business requirements — pick when a requirement set must be made rigorous and spec-ready before specification
