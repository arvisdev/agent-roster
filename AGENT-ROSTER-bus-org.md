# AGENT ROSTER (bus-org)

Business organization — intent, requirements, risk, and compliance. These agents  
turn stakeholder intent into clear, testable, compliance-checked requirements.  

Behavioral Specification Specialist  
Agent: https://github.com/arvisdev/agent-roster/tree/main/bus-org/behavioral-specification-specialist  
Description: Turns agreed requirements into independently testable behavior specs — acceptance criteria, BDD/Gherkin scenarios, and requirements-to-test traceability — pick when a requirement must become a testable specification  
Model: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash  
Image: hermes-agent  

Business Requirements Specialist  
Agent: https://github.com/arvisdev/agent-roster/tree/main/bus-org/business-requirements-specialist  
Description: Business Analyst who elicits, documents, and validates business requirements (objectives, scope, constraints, success measures) and gets them ready for solution specification — pick when a business-need statement must be made rigorous before detailed specification  
Model: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash  
Image: hermes-agent
